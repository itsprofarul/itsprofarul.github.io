About

Dr. Arul Kumar Natarajan is Professor and Program Head of Cybersecurity in the School of Computing at Samarkand International University of Technology (SIUT), Uzbekistan, where he has served since January 2024. He leads curriculum development, program delivery, and research mentorship for the cybersecurity program.

He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Bharathidasan University, India, in 2017, and completed postdoctoral research at the Singapore Institute of Technology (2024–2025) on an ensemble framework for detecting URL-based phishing attacks using generative AI techniques.

With over 15 years of academic and research experience, he has served at CHRIST (Deemed to be University) and Bishop Heber College in India, and Debre Berhan University in Ethiopia. He has authored 73 Scopus-indexed scholarly works, written academic books with Springer Nature, edited 10 Scopus-indexed books, and holds 18 patents in India and the UK along with a registered copyright in India.
