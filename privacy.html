Privacy Policy

For the personal WhatsApp bot and Telegram bot (@itsprofarul_bot) operated by Dr. Arul Kumar Natarajan
Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Who operates these bots

These bots are personal, non-commercial assistants operated by Dr. Arul Kumar Natarajan, Professor & Program Head (Cybersecurity), Samarkand International University of Technology, Uzbekistan. They share public information about his academic profile, research, website, and publications.

2. Who can use them

Access is restricted to the owner and a small list of approved phone numbers or accounts. Messages from anyone else are ignored and receive no reply.

3. Information processed

When an approved user sends a message, the bot receives the sender's phone number (WhatsApp) or user ID (Telegram) and the message text, solely to decide which reply to send. The bot handles text commands only.

4. Information stored

  • Message content is not stored. Messages are processed in memory and discarded after the reply is sent.
  • The bot keeps only simple usage counts (for example, number of messages handled) in memory. These counts are erased whenever the bot restarts.
  • Server logs show at most the last four digits of a phone number, for troubleshooting.
  • No data is written to a database or file, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

5. Third-party platforms

Messages travel through WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.) and Telegram, which process data under their own privacy policies: WhatsApp Privacy Policy and Telegram Privacy Policy.

6. Security

Access tokens and secrets are kept out of source code. Every incoming WhatsApp webhook is verified with Meta's HMAC-SHA256 signature, and only allowlisted senders are answered.

7. Data deletion

Because no personal data is retained, there is normally nothing to delete. To ask a question or request confirmation, send a message to the bot or use the contact details on itsprofarul.github.io; requests are answered within 30 days.

8. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
